Honeypot Intelligence Knowledge Site

Honeypot Intelligence is a research platform built around a contained SSH honeypot (a decoy server that records what attackers do; sometimes glossed as a "bal kupu" in Turkish). It runs Cowrie on tcp/22 of a rented virtual server, records every login attempt, command and uploaded file, analyses them with a FastAPI backend and shows them in a React SOC dashboard. Strict egress containment guarantees that the honeypot can never be used against third parties.

This site is the public, curated view of that project. The numbers on the statistics pages come from a production snapshot taken on 2026-10-21; engineering facts come from the project documents and carry their truth level (PLANNED, IMPLEMENTED, REVIEWED, VALIDATED, MEASURED, UNKNOWN) and date.
