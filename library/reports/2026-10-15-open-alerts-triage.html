Group B - filter probe (9 medium) #

All 9 sessions run exactly the known 5-command probe (PATH export, printf ... > filter, run, delete), client SSH-2.0-Go, nothing else. Sources: AS47890 UNMANAGED-DEDICATED-SERVERS (92.118.39.14/.49/.50, 80.94.92.55/.234, 2.57.122.74/.209) and, new for this group, AS48090 DMZHOST - TECHOFF SRV LIMITED (195.178.110.228, .232). Detection still yields about one medium alert per IP per day; the planned tuning (TODO.md) is still open.
